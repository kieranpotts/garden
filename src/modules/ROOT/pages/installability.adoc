= Installability

*Installability* is the degree of effectiveness and efficiency with which a product or system can be successfully
installed, and/or uninstalled, in a specified environment. It's a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*],
and specifically a sub-characteristic of xref:portability.adoc[*portability*] in the ISO/IEC 25010 quality model.

A highly installable system requires little manual intervention or specialist knowledge to set up correctly, fails
predictably and informatively when it can't be installed, and can be removed again without leaving the environment
in a clean state.

These concerns matter well beyond desktop software. Automated deployment pipelines, container images, and
infrastructure-as-code all depend on their targets being reliably and repeatably installable.

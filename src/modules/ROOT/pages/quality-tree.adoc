= Quality tree

A *quality tree*, also called a *quality attribute utility tree*, is a hierarchical model that decomposes a system's
overall xref:software-quality.adoc[*software quality*] into successively refined branches. Top-level
xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attributes*] are refined into more specific sub-characteristics, which are
themselves broken down further, down to concrete xref:scenario.adoc[*scenarios*] at the leaves.

Standards for product quality, such as ISO/IEC 25010, propose a generic quality tree applicable to any software
product, defining characteristics like reliability, performance efficiency, and maintainability, and their
sub-characteristics. The quality tree for a specific system narrows this generic model down to the
xref:quality-requirement.adoc[*quality requirements*] that actually matter for that system, prioritized against its
own context and stakeholders.

Because its leaves are concrete and testable, a quality tree is a useful tool in
xref:architecture-evaluation.adoc[*architecture evaluation*] methods, such as the
xref:architecture-tradeoff-analysis-method.adoc[*Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method*] (ATAM), where the tree
structures discussion of trade-offs among competing qualities.

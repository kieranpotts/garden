= Continuous integration

Continuous integration is a software development *[practice]* where members of a team integrate
their work at regular intervals, typically at least once a day.

It is realized through xref:commit-early-commit-often.adoc[*commit early, commit often*], the habit
of saving work in small, frequent increments.

Continuous integration is a prerequisite for xref:continuous-delivery.adoc[*continuous delivery*]
and xref:continuous-deployment.adoc[*continuous deployment*].

Continuous integration is one of the primary practices of
xref:extreme-programming.adoc[*Extreme Programming (XP)*], which prescribes integrating and testing
changes after no more than a couple of hours.

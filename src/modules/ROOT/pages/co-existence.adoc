= Co-existence

:link-iso25010: https://iso25000.com/index.php/en/iso-25000-standards/iso-25010

*Co-existence* is the degree to which a product can perform its required functions efficiently while sharing a common
environment and resources with other products, without any detrimental impact on those other products. It is a
xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and, per ISO/IEC 25010, a sub-characteristic of
xref:compatibility.adoc[*compatibility*].

In practice, co-existence matters whenever multiple independent systems run on the same host, network, or platform.
For example, several applications sharing a server's CPU, memory, or disk, or several services competing for the same
downstream dependency. A system with good co-existence degrades gracefully under that kind of contention, rather than
starving its neighbors of resources.

= Accountability

*Accountability* is the degree to which the actions of an entity — a user, a service, a process — can be traced
uniquely back to that entity. It's a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*], and ISO/IEC 25010 classes it as
a sub-characteristic of xref:security.adoc[*security*].

Systems achieve accountability primarily through xref:logging.adoc[*logging*] and audit trails that record who did
what and when, combined with strong xref:authentication.adoc[*authentication*] so that a recorded action can be
attributed to a real, verified identity, rather than a shared or spoofed one.

xref:traceability.adoc[*Traceability*] is the capacity to follow that chain of evidence from an outcome back to its
cause.

Accountability is also the basis for xref:non-repudiation.adoc[*non-repudiation*]. Once an action is accountable and
irrefutably attributed, the entity that performed it cannot credibly deny having done so.

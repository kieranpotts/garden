= Telemetry

Telemetry is the automatic in situ recording of data from a system, and its
transmission to a receiving station from where the data can be
*xref:monitoring.adoc[monitored]* and analyzed (eg. using
*xref:observability.adoc[observability]* tools).

Telemetry data can be gathered from different sources, to monitor and observe
different aspects of a system, and depending on the observability strategy. See
the *xref:monitoring.adoc[monitoring]* and
*xref:observability.adoc[observability]* sections for examples.

The three conventional telemetry signals are *xref:logging.adoc[logs]*,
*xref:metrics.adoc[metrics]*, and *xref:tracing.adoc[traces]*, the three pillars
of *xref:observability.adoc[observability]*.

The same telemetry also feeds *xref:alerting.adoc[alerting]* systems, which
trigger notifications when values cross defined thresholds.

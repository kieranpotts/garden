= Cardinality

*Cardinality* describes the quantitative rating of an xref:association.adoc[*association*] or xref:relationship.adoc[*relationship*]
between elements in a system or model. It specifies how many participants — objects, instances, modules, or other elements —
take part in that association, typically expressed as a range such as one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many.

Cardinality is most commonly encountered in data modeling and object-oriented design, where it constrains how many
instances of one type may be associated with instances of another. It is a structural property of the relationship
itself, independent of the behavior of the participants it connects.

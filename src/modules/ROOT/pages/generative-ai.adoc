Generative AI refers to xref:machine-learning.adoc[*machine learning*] systems that produce new
content – text, images, video, audio, 3D models, code, and more – rather than simply classifying or
analyzing existing data. These systems are trained on large datasets and learn to generate plausible
new outputs by modeling the statistical patterns in that data.

xref:large-language-model.adoc[*Large language models (LLMs)*] are the most prominent example,
generating text and code. But generative AI encompasses other modalities too.

Fundamentally, these models are all developed in much the same way — trained on large datasets to
model the statistical patterns in that data. What differs is mainly the type of media they are
trained on and the architecture best suited to it. Just as LLMs specialize in text,
*diffusion models* specialize in image generation (see below).

This section covers the broader landscape of generative AI, including image, video, and audio
generation.

== Image generation

Image generation models produce photorealistic or stylized images from text prompts, reference
images, or other inputs. Most modern image generators are based on diffusion models, which learn to
iteratively de-noise random noise into coherent images.

Notable tools and platforms include:

* {link-midjourney}[Midjourney]: High-quality image generation accessible via Discord and
  web interface.

* {link-gpt-image}[GPT Image]: OpenAI's current,
  natively-integrated image generation model (gpt-image-1), noted for near-perfect text rendering.
  The successor to DALL·E.

* {link-dalle}[DALL·E]: OpenAI's earlier image generation line, integrated into
  ChatGPT.

* {link-stable-diffusion}[Stable Diffusion]: Open-weight diffusion model from Stability AI, widely
  used as a base for fine-tuned variants.

* {link-flux}[Flux]: High-resolution, prompt-editable, open-weight image models from
  Black Forest Labs.

* {link-ideogram}[Ideogram]: Image generation noted for rendering precise, legible text within
  images.

* {link-adobe-firefly}[Adobe Firefly]: Adobe's generative image models,
  integrated into Creative Cloud and designed to be commercially safe.

* {link-comfyui}[ComfyUI]: Open-source, node-based visual workflow engine for running
  diffusion models locally, supporting image, video, 3D, and audio generation pipelines.

== Video generation

Video generation models extend image generation into the temporal dimension, producing short clips
from text or image prompts.

Notable tools and platforms include:

* {link-sora}[Sora]: OpenAI's text-to-video model.

* {link-google-veo}[Google Veo]: Google DeepMind's text-to-video model, with
  synchronized audio generation.

* {link-kling}[Kling]: Cinematic, realistic video generation, from Kuaishou.

* {link-runway}[Runway]: Commercial video generation and editing platform.

* {link-luma}[Luma Dream Machine]: Text-to-video and image-to-video generation.

== Audio generation

Audio generation covers music, sound effects, and voice synthesis from text or other audio inputs.

Notable tools and platforms include:

* {link-suno}[Suno]: AI music generation from text prompts.

* {link-udio}[Udio]: Music generation platform.

* {link-elevenlabs}[ElevenLabs]: Voice synthesis and cloning, widely used for narration and
  dubbing.

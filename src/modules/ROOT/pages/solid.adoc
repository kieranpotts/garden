= SOLID principles

SOLID is an acronym for five *xref:design-principles.adoc[design principles]* of
*xref:object-oriented-programming.adoc[object-oriented programming]*, introduced
by Robert C. Martin. The five principles are the
*xref:single-responsibility-principle.adoc[single responsibility principle]*, the
*xref:open-closed-principle.adoc[open-closed principle]*, the
*xref:liskov-substitution-principle.adoc[Liskov substitution principle]*, the
*xref:interface-segregation-principle.adoc[interface segregation principle]*, and
*xref:dependency-inversion.adoc[dependency inversion]*.

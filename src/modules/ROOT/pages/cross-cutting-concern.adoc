= Cross-cutting concern

A cross-cutting concern is an aspect of a system's design that affects multiple
components, layers, or services, rather than being localized to one part of the
codebase. *xref:non-functional-requirements.adoc[Non-functional requirements]* –
performance, security, availability, and the like – are the classic example:
satisfying one typically means touching many parts of the system at once.

= Access patterns

A key consideration in *xref:system-design.adoc[system design]* is the access
patterns of the data.

Access patterns describe how data is read and written, and how it is queried.
Systems may be read-heavy or write-heavy, or have a balanced mix of reads and
writes. The access patterns of a system are influenced by its
*xref:domain.adoc[domain]* and *xref:requirements.adoc[requirements]* such as
*xref:scalability.adoc[scalability]*, *xref:availability.adoc[availability]*,
and *xref:consistency.adoc[consistency]*.

Access patterns can have a significant influence on the
*xref:architecture-and-design.adoc[design]* of a system, but the patterns may
not be fully understood until the system is in production. Therefore, optimizing
the system design for its access patterns is a challenging constraint and may
require iterative design improvements.

= Usability

*Usability* is the degree to which a product or system can be used by specified users to achieve specified goals with
effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction, in a specified context of use. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*]
and one of the eight top-level characteristics in the ISO/IEC 25010 quality model, alongside others such as
xref:performance-efficiency.adoc[*performance efficiency*] and xref:maintainability.adoc[*maintainability*].

ISO/IEC 25010 decomposes usability into six sub-characteristics: xref:appropriateness.adoc[*appropriateness recognizability*],
whether users can tell the product fits their needs; xref:learnability.adoc[*learnability*], how
readily a new user builds a working mental model of it; xref:operability.adoc[*operability*], how easy it is to run and
control; xref:user-error-protection.adoc[*user error protection*], how well it guards against mistakes;
xref:user-interface-aesthetics.adoc[*user interface aesthetics*], how pleasing the interaction is; and
xref:accessibility.adoc[*accessibility*], how well it serves users with the widest range of characteristics and
capabilities.

Because it's framed around the end user's experience rather than a system's internal qualities, usability is usually
assessed through xref:usability-testing.adoc[*usability testing*] with real or representative users, rather than through
the automated checks that suit attributes like performance or security.

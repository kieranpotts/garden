= Connascence

// TODO: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connascence

*Connascence* is a software quality metric that describes different levels and
dimensions of *xref:coupling.adoc[coupling]*. Two components are connascent when a
change in one would require a change in the other as well. Lower connascence
means higher code quality. The concept generalizes and extends the older
*xref:coupling.adoc[coupling]* and *xref:cohesion.adoc[cohesion]* ideas, and was
formalized by Meilir Page-Jones.

Each instance of connascence is considered on three axes, namely strength,
degree, and locality. *Strength* is the type of the connascence, ranked from
weak to strong. Weaker connascence is preferable because it is easier to change.
*Degree* is how many occurrences of the connascence exist between the components.
*Locality* is how far apart the components are. Connascence is worse when
components are far apart.

Connascence gives developers a vocabulary to talk about different types of
coupling. The types, in increasing strength, are *Connascence of Name*,
*Connascence of Type*, *Connascence of Meaning*, *Connascence of Position*,
*Connascence of Algorithm*, *Connascence of Execution*, and *Connascence of
Timing*. Stronger connascence is harder to change.

The weakest form, *Connascence of Name*, requires only that two components agree
on the name of something. Renaming a method forces every caller to be updated,
but nothing else about the callers need change. *Connascence of Position*,
further up the scale, requires that components agree on the order of elements,
such as the positional arguments to a function. A change in the order forces a
matching change at every call site and is correspondingly more disruptive.

The practical value of connascence is as a guide to
*xref:decoupling.adoc[decoupling]*. Stronger forms can often be refactored into
weaker ones. Passing values by position can be replaced by passing them by name.
Hidden assumptions captured by *Connascence of Meaning* – where two components
must agree on the interpretation of a value – can be made explicit through
*xref:cohesion.adoc[cohesive]* types. Reducing the strength of connascence is a
concrete way to honor the *xref:law-of-demeter.adoc[Law of Demeter]* and other
*xref:design-principles.adoc[design principles]*.

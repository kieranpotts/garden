= Port

A *port* is a xref:uml.adoc[*UML*] construct, used on component diagrams, that defines a distinct point of interaction
between a xref:component.adoc[*component*] and its environment. Rather than exposing a single, undifferentiated
boundary, a component can declare several ports, each one grouping the specific xref:interface.adoc[*interfaces*] –
provided, required, or both – that are relevant to one particular collaboration.

Separating a component's interaction points into ports lets a diagram show precisely which interfaces are needed to
fulfil which role, and lets two components be wired together port-to-port through a connector, without either side
needing to know anything about the other beyond the interfaces exposed at that port.

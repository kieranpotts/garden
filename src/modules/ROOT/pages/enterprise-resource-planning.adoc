= Enterprise resource planning (ERP)

Enterprise resource planning refers to the management of an organization's resources – such as cash, raw materials, production capacity, and people – and the status of business commitments – such as orders and payroll.

IT systems may automate many aspects of ERP. ERP software is a category of *xref:management-information-systems.adoc[management information systems (MIS)]*, which itself is a category of *xref:enterprise-application-software.adoc[enterprise application software]*.

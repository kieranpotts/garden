= Testability

*Testability* is the degree of effectiveness and efficiency with which *test criteria* can be established for a system,
product, or component, and with which tests can then be performed to determine whether those criteria have been met.
It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and, per ISO/IEC 25010, a sub-characteristic of
xref:maintainability.adoc[*maintainability*]. A system that's hard to test is, by the same token, hard to change with
confidence.

A system's testability is shaped largely by its design rather than by its tests. High xref:cohesion.adoc[*cohesion*]
and low xref:coupling.adoc[*coupling*] let a component be exercised in isolation, without dragging in the rest of the
system as fixtures or mocks. xref:dependency-injection.adoc[*Dependency injection*] and programming to an
xref:interface.adoc[*interface*] make it possible to substitute real collaborators with *test doubles*, and observable,
deterministic behavior — pure functions, explicit inputs and outputs, no hidden global state — makes test criteria
easier to state in the first place.

Testability is what makes disciplines such as xref:test-driven-development.adoc[*test-driven development*] and
xref:unit-testing.adoc[*unit testing*] practical at scale. Where a codebase resists testing, the usual remedy is
xref:refactoring.adoc[*refactoring*] toward smaller, more loosely coupled units rather than writing ever more elaborate
test setup around the existing structure.

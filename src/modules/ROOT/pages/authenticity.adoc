= Authenticity

*Authenticity* is the degree to which the identity of a subject or resource can be proven to be the one it claims to
be. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and, per ISO/IEC 25010, a sub-characteristic of
xref:security.adoc[*security*], alongside confidentiality, integrity, xref:non-repudiation.adoc[*non-repudiation*], and
xref:accountability.adoc[*accountability*].

A system establishes authenticity through mechanisms such as xref:authentication.adoc[*authentication*] credentials,
digital signatures, and certificates, each of which ties a claimed identity to evidence that only the genuine subject
could plausibly produce. Without it, a system has no reliable basis for trusting who or what it is communicating
with, which undermines every other security control built on top of that trust.

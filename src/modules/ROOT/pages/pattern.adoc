= Pattern

A *pattern* is a reusable, well-documented solution to a problem that recurs in a particular context, generalized
enough to be reapplied across many specific instances of that problem rather than solving it once. Recording a
pattern captures not just the solution but the trade-offs behind it, so it can be recognized and reused by others
facing the same problem.

In software, the term most often refers to a xref:design-pattern.adoc[*design pattern*] — a proven solution to a
recurring problem in object-oriented design, at the level of classes and their collaborations — or an
xref:architectural-pattern.adoc[*architectural pattern*], the equivalent at the level of a whole system's structure.
The same underlying idea, a named and reusable solution with known trade-offs, recurs at other scales too, from
*idioms* at the level of a single line of code to xref:anti-pattern.adoc[*anti-patterns*], which document a
commonly reached-for solution that turns out to cause more problems than it solves.

= User interface aesthetics

*User interface aesthetics* is the degree to which a xref:user-interface.adoc[*user interface*] enables pleasing and
satisfying interaction for the user. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and, per ISO/IEC 25010, a
sub-characteristic of xref:usability.adoc[*usability*], alongside others such as xref:learnability.adoc[*learnability*]
and xref:accessibility.adoc[*accessibility*].

Unlike most of usability's other sub-characteristics, aesthetics is concerned with subjective satisfaction rather than
with a user's ability to complete a task. An interface can be fully operable and still feel unpleasant to use, and a
beautiful one can still fail at its job.

In practice, the two are related. A well-designed visual hierarchy, consistent styling, and appropriate use of
*whitespace* and typography tend to make an interface both more pleasing and easier to learn.

= Architecture and design

*xref:non-functional-requirements.adoc[non-functional requirements]*, which
determine the system's *xref:quality-attributes.adoc[quality attributes]* such
as its *xref:availability.adoc[availability]*,
*xref:performance.adoc[performance]*, *xref:scalability.adoc[scalability]*, and
*xref:security.adoc[security]*.

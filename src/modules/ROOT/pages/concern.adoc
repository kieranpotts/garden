= Concern

A *concern* is an interest that a xref:stakeholder.adoc[*stakeholder*] has in a system, relevant to how that system is
architected. It may take the form of a xref:requirement.adoc[*requirement*], an objective, a xref:constraint.adoc[*constraint*],
an intention, or simply an aspiration – whatever it is that a stakeholder wants, needs, or expects from the architecture.

Concerns are not limited to functional or technical matters. ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010, the standard that formalizes the term,
treats a concern as pertaining to any influence on a system within its environment, including developmental,
technological, business, operational, organizational, political, economic, legal, regulatory, ecological, and social
influences.

Identifying and reconciling the concerns of different stakeholders – who often want conflicting things from the same
system – is a core activity of *software architecture*.

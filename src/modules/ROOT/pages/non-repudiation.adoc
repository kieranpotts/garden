= Non-repudiation

*Non-repudiation* is the degree to which an action or event can be proven to have taken place, such that the party
responsible for it cannot credibly deny it afterwards. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and,
per ISO/IEC 25010, a sub-characteristic of xref:security.adoc[*security*].

In practice, non-repudiation is achieved through mechanisms that bind an action to its originator in a way a third
party can verify, such as a digital xref:signature.adoc[*signature*] or an audit log entry cryptographically linked
to a xref:principal.adoc[*principal*]'s identity.

It's distinct from xref:authenticity.adoc[*authenticity*], which establishes that data or a message genuinely comes
from a claimed source. Authenticity is about identifying who acted, while non-repudiation is about making that
identification stand up as proof later, even if the party disputes it.

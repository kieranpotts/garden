= Computer science

Computer science is the study of computation: what can be computed, how
efficiently, and how to build systems that compute. It spans both theory —
*xref:algorithms.adoc[algorithms]*, *xref:complexity.adoc[complexity]*, and
*xref:formal-methods.adoc[formal methods]* — and practice, from
*xref:programming-paradigm.adoc[programming paradigms]* and
*xref:data-structures.adoc[data structures]* to
*xref:distributed-system.adoc[distributed systems]* and
*xref:artificial-intelligence.adoc[artificial intelligence]*. The boundary with
software engineering is not sharp. Computer science asks what is possible and
how; software engineering asks how to reliably deliver it. This page collects
the garden's entries on core computer science topics.

= Notation

A *notation* is a system of marks, signs, symbols, or characters used to represent information in a consistent,
shareable form. In software engineering, a notation gives a xref:model.adoc[*model*] or a piece of documentation a
precise, agreed vocabulary, so that what one person draws or writes can be read unambiguously by another.

Notations range from plain prose and structured text — tables, bullet lists, numbered lists — to formal visual
languages purpose-built for describing software and processes, such as xref:uml.adoc[*UML*] for object-oriented
design and xref:bpmn.adoc[*BPMN*] for business processes.

Choosing a notation is a trade-off between expressiveness and how widely it's understood. A rich, formal notation says
more precisely but demands more of the reader, while prose is universally readable but harder to check for consistency.

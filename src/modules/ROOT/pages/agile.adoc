= Agile software development

Agile software development is an umbrella term for a family of software
development approaches that share a common set of values and principles.
They are codified in the _Manifesto for Agile Software Development_ (the
"Agile Manifesto"), and favor
*xref:iterative-and-incremental-development.adoc[iterative and incremental development]*,
collaboration between motivated people, and a willingness to adapt to
changing circumstances over the plan-driven, sequential working styles
associated with the *xref:waterfall-model.adoc[waterfall model]* and
*xref:big-design-up-front.adoc[big design up-front]*.

The manifesto was written in February 2001, when seventeen software
developers met at a ski lodge in Snowbird, Utah. The group brought together
the originators and proponents of several then-emerging development methods,
including *xref:scrum.adoc[Scrum]*,
*xref:extreme-programming.adoc[Extreme Programming (XP)]*,
*xref:adaptive-software-development.adoc[adaptive software development]*,
*xref:crystal-clear.adoc[Crystal Clear]*, and *xref:dsdm.adoc[DSDM]*. They
coined the term "agile" to describe what those methods shared, and published
the manifesto at https://agilemanifesto.org/[agilemanifesto.org].

== Values

The manifesto frames four value statements. Each is an ordering of
priorities, not a rejection of the right-hand side. The authors explicitly
noted that, while there is value in the items on the right, they value the
items on the left more.

.Core agile values
****
* Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
* Working software over comprehensive documentation
* Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
* Responding to change over following a plan
****

== Principles

Behind the four values, the authors enumerated twelve principles that
flesh them out. The principles call for early and continuous delivery of
working software, welcoming changing requirements even late in development,
and treating working software as the primary measure of progress. They
emphasize face-to-face conversation as the most effective way to convey
information within a team, and self-organizing teams as the source of the
best architectures and designs. They also call for a sustainable pace,
continuous attention to technical excellence, regular reflection, and
simplicity – "the art of maximizing the amount of work not done."

Technical excellence, in particular, is what separates effective agile
delivery from mere ceremony. It draws on practices such as
*xref:test-driven-development.adoc[test-driven development]*,
*xref:continuous-integration.adoc[continuous integration]*, and
*xref:refactoring.adoc[refactoring]*. Without these, teams can go through
the motions of an agile process while accumulating
*xref:technical-debt.adoc[technical debt]* that erodes their ability to
respond to change – the dysfunction Martin Fowler dubbed
*xref:flaccid-agile.adoc[flaccid agile]*. Agile adopted superficially, as
process theater rather than a set of working values, is a form of
*xref:cargo-culting.adoc[cargo-culting]*.

== Methods

Agile is not a single method but a family. Scrum prescribes time-boxed
sprints and specific ceremonies. Extreme Programming prescribes technical
practices. *xref:kanban.adoc[Kanban]*, which predates the agile movement but
is widely classed with it, prescribes flow and work-in-progress limits
rather than iterations. Other members of the family include Crystal Clear,
adaptive software development,
*xref:feature-driven-development.adoc[feature-driven development]*, DSDM,
and *xref:lean-software-development.adoc[lean software development]*. In
practice, most teams blend elements from several methods into their own
*xref:ways-of-working.adoc[ways of working]* rather than following one
verbatim.

== Craftsmanship

The *xref:software-craftsmanship.adoc[software craftsmanship]* movement
extends the agile values with a stronger focus on technical mastery and
professionalism. Its 2009 manifesto builds directly on the Agile Manifesto,
adding craft, community, and partnership to the original four values.

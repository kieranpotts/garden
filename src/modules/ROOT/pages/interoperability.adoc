= Interoperability

*Interoperability* is the degree to which two or more systems, products, or components can exchange information and go
on to make use of the information that has been exchanged.

It is not enough for one system to send data another can receive. Interoperability also requires that the receiving
system understands the data well enough to act on it.

ISO/IEC 25010 classifies interoperability as a sub-characteristic of xref:compatibility.adoc[*compatibility*], the
broader xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] that also covers xref:co-existence.adoc[*co-existence*], or a
system's ability to share an environment with others without interfering with them. Interoperability, by contrast, is
about deliberate information exchange between systems, rather than passive resource sharing.

Open standards and well-specified xref:interface.adoc[*interfaces*] are the usual route to interoperability, since they
let independently built systems agree on a contract without either side depending on the other's internals. This is
part of why formats such as xref:json.adoc[*JSON*] and protocols such as xref:http.adoc[*HTTP*] are so widely adopted.
They give unrelated systems a common open language to exchange information in.

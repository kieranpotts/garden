= Appropriateness

*Appropriateness*, also called *adequacy*, is the general quality of being suitable for a particular purpose. In software
quality specifically, ISO/IEC 25010 splits the idea into two more precise, named sub-characteristics rather than
treating it as a single measurable attribute.

*Appropriateness recognizability* is the degree to which users can recognize whether a product or system is
appropriate for their needs, and is a sub-characteristic of xref:usability.adoc[*usability*]. It's about the product
signalling its fit for purpose to a prospective user, not about that fit being real.

*Functional appropriateness* is the degree to which the product's functions actually facilitate the accomplishment of
specified tasks and objectives. It is a sub-characteristic of *functional suitability* alongside
xref:completeness.adoc[*functional completeness*] and xref:correctness.adoc[*functional correctness*].

Both are xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attributes*] under the same ISO/IEC 25010 model, but they answer
different questions. Recognizability is about perception before use, appropriateness is about fit during use.

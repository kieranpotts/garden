= Heterogeneous architectural style

A *heterogeneous architectural style* is a system xref:architectural-pattern.adoc[*architecture*] that is composed from
two or more distinct *architectural styles or patterns*, rather than applying a single style uniformly throughout. It's
a synonym for the xref:hybrid-architecture-style.adoc[*hybrid architecture style*], and the two terms are used
interchangeably.

Most non-trivial real-world systems are heterogeneous. A single style rarely suits every part of a system equally well,
so different subsystems or layers are shaped by whichever style best fits their own concerns. Thus, the overall
architecture is a composite of those individual choices.

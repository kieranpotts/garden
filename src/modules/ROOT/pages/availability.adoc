= Availability (aka. reliability)

// TODO: https://blog.algomaster.io/p/system-design-what-is-availability

Availability is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] of an IT system that refers to the proportion of time
that a system remains operational and accessible to users, even under heavy load and even when parts of the system fail.

Availability is closely related to xref:scalability.adoc[*scalability*] and
xref:fault-tolerance.adoc[*fault tolerance*]. In cloud platforms it is commonly achieved by replicating workloads across
xref:availability-zone.adoc[*availability zones*].

Availability is typically expressed as a percentage of xref:uptime.adoc[*uptime*] over a given period. For example, a
system that has an uptime of 99.9% is considered to have "three nines of availability".

[Attributes]
|===
|Availability %
|Downtime per year

|99% ("two nines")
|3.65 days

|99.9% ("three nines")
|8.76 hours

|99.99% ("four nines")
|52.56 minutes

|99.999% ("five nines")
|5.26 minutes

|99.9999% ("six nines")
|31.5 seconds
|===

What is considered to be "highly available" depends on the system's xref:requirement.adoc[*requirements*]. A system with
three nines of availability would actually have a xref:downtime.adoc[*downtime*] of 8.76 hours per year, which may be
acceptable for some systems but not for others. 99.99% uptime (four nines) would mean a downtime of 52.56 minutes per
year. These percentage targets are the language of xref:service-level-agreement.adoc[*service level agreements*], where
a provider commits to keeping availability above a stated threshold over a billing period and faces remedies when it
does not.

// The two main components of availability are xref:failover.adoc[*failover*] and xref:replication.adoc[*replication*] (or xref:redundancy.adoc[*redundancy*]). Failover is the ability of a system to switch to a backup component in the event of a failure, while replication is the ability to create multiple copies of a system or its components to ensure that there is always a backup available. Both of the qualities in combination are required to delivery high availability.

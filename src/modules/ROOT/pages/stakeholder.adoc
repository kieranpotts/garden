= Stakeholder

A *stakeholder* is any individual, team, or organization with an interest in a xref:system.adoc[*system*], its
development, or its operation. Per ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010, that interest need not be favorable. It can be positive, as
when a stakeholder wants to benefit from the system; neutral, as when a stakeholder is only required to test or
verify it; or negative, as when a stakeholder competes with the system or wants it to fail.

Stakeholders extend well beyond a system's end users. The category also covers employees, owners, and
administrators who depend on the system operationally; the xref:requirements-engineering.adoc[*requirements engineers*]
and business analysts who elicit and shape what it should do; the developers, designers, and architects
who build it; project and product managers and product owners who steer its direction; and external parties such as
government agencies with a regulatory interest.

Because stakeholders' interests routinely conflict — a regulator's concerns are rarely a developer's, and a
competitor's never align with either — identifying the relevant stakeholders for a system and reconciling what each
of them needs from it is treated as a distinct, early activity in requirements engineering and architecture
description work, rather than something to discover incidentally along the way.

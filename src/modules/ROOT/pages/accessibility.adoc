= Accessibility

Accessibility is the degree to which a product or system can be used by people with the widest range of
characteristics and capabilities — including disabilities — to achieve a specified goal in a specified context of
use. It's a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*], and ISO/IEC 25010 classes it as a sub-characteristic of
xref:usability.adoc[*usability*].

Accessibility work typically targets specific categories of barrier: visual (contrast, screen-reader compatibility),
motor (keyboard-only operation, tap-target sizing), auditory (captions, transcripts), and cognitive (plain language,
predictable navigation). Standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) translate the general
principle into testable criteria for particular technologies.

= Time behavior

*Time behavior* is the degree to which the response times, processing times, and throughput rates of a product or system,
when performing its functions, meet requirements. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and, per
ISO/IEC 25010, one of three sub-characteristics of xref:performance-efficiency.adoc[*performance efficiency*], alongside
xref:resource-utilization.adoc[*resource utilization*] and xref:capacity.adoc[*capacity*].

Time behavior is usually the sub-characteristic people mean when they talk informally about a system's
xref:performance.adoc[*performance*], or how quickly a request is served, how long a batch job takes to complete, and
how many operations a system can sustain per unit of time. It's typically the target of
xref:performance-testing.adoc[*performance testing*], xref:load-testing.adoc[*load testing*], and
xref:stress-testing.adoc[*stress testing*], which measure xref:latency.adoc[*latency*] and
xref:throughput.adoc[*throughput*] under varying conditions to check they meet their specified requirements.

= Heuristic

A *heuristic* is an informal rule-of-thumb is *software design*.

Software design draws on heuristics constantly, from object-oriented design guidelines such as "favor composition
over inheritance" to user-interface heuristics such as Jakob Nielsen's usability guidelines.

Unlike a xref:design-principle.adoc[*design principle*], which is usually justified by an underlying rationale about
why it tends to hold, a heuristic's value lies in narrowing the space of options quickly, rather than settling a
design question conclusively.

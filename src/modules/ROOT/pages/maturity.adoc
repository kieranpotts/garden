= Maturity

As a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*], *maturity* is the degree to which a system, product, or component
meets its needs for xref:reliability.adoc[*reliability*] under normal operation. In other words, how consistently it
performs its specified functions without failing, once the usual early-life defects have been found and fixed.

ISO/IEC 25010 classifies maturity as a sub-characteristic of reliability, alongside xref:availability.adoc[*availability*],
xref:fault-tolerance.adoc[*fault tolerance*], and xref:recoverability.adoc[*recoverability*]. Where those three describe
how a system copes with, and bounces back from, failure, maturity describes the more basic property of how rarely it fails
in the first place under expected conditions. A mature system has already had its obvious defects shaken out through use.

The term is distinct from a xref:capability-maturity-model.adoc[*capability maturity model*], which measures the
maturity of an organization's processes, rather than that of a running system's behavior.

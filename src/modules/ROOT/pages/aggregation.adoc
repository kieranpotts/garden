= Aggregation

*Aggregation* is a "has-a" relationship between objects in xref:object-oriented-programming.adoc[*object-oriented programming*],
in which one object holds references to others without owning their lifecycle.

It's a weaker form of xref:composition.adoc[*composition*]. Both describe one object being built from others, but
aggregation doesn't imply that the contained objects belong exclusively to the containing one.

The distinguishing test is lifecycle dependency. In composition, the contained parts are destroyed along with the
whole that owns them. For example, an order's line items don't outlive the order. In aggregation, destroying the
containing object leaves the contained objects intact, because they exist independently and may be shared with, or
referenced by, other objects at the same time. For example, disbanding a university department doesn't destroy its
tutors.

Aggregation and composition are both specializations of xref:association.adoc[*association*], the more general
xref:uml.adoc[*UML*] term for a structural relationship between classes. What differs between the three is only the
strength of ownership implied.

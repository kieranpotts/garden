= Memoization

Memoization is a *xref:design-patterns.adoc[design pattern]* in which the result
of a function's execution is cached, and the cached result is returned when the
function is subsequently called with identical inputs, thus avoiding redundant
computation.

Memoization is a form of *xref:caching.adoc[caching]*, but it is specifically
used to optimize the performance of functions that are computationally expensive
or time-consuming. It is the basis of the dynamic programming approach to
*xref:algorithms.adoc[algorithm]* design, where overlapping subproblems are
solved once and their results reused.

Memoization is related to xref:lazy-loading.adoc[*lazy loading*], which
likewise defers work until it is needed — though lazy loading defers the loading
of a resource, while memoization caches the result of a computation.

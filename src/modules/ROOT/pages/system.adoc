= System

A *system* is a collection of elements — *building blocks*, components, or other parts — organized together to serve a
common purpose.

The term is deliberately broad. ISO/IEC 15288 defines systems as man-made, and possibly configured from any mix of hardware,
software, data, humans, processes, procedures, facilities, and materials, while IEEE Std 1471:2000 extends the idea to any
*software-intensive system* — one where software contributes essential influence to its design, construction, deployment,
and evolution — to cover individual applications, subsystems, whole enterprises, and systems of systems alike.

"System" is most often shorthand for a xref:software-system.adoc[*software system*], the thing whose architecture, quality,
and behavior the rest of these entries are concerned with.

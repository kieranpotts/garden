= Service

A *service* is a technical, autonomous unit that bundles related functionality, typically scoped to a single topic or
capability, and exposes it to other xref:building-block.adoc[*building blocks*] through a well-defined
xref:interface.adoc[*interface*].

A well-designed service abstracts its internal implementation to such a degree that a consumer need not know or understand
how the service works internally in order to use it.

Web services, network services, operating system services, and telecommunications services are all familiar examples of
externally accessible services.

Within xref:domain-driven-design.adoc[*domain-driven design*], *service* takes on a more specific meaning. It refers to
a stateless building block used to implement business logic or processes that don't naturally belong to any single
xref:entity.adoc[*entity*], xref:aggregate.adoc[*aggregate*], or xref:value-object.adoc[*value object*], or which act
across several of them at once. Because a *domain service* holds no state of its own, its operations take *domain objects*
as parameters and return domain objects as results, rather than accumulating any internal data between calls.

The general and DDD senses of *service* both describe the same underlying idea – a unit of behavior, hidden behind an
interface, that other parts of a system can call without depending on its internals – at different scales. That same
idea, applied to whole systems composed from many independently deployable services, is what
xref:service-oriented-architecture.adoc[*service-oriented architecture*] and its finer-grained descendant,
xref:microservices.adoc[*microservices*], are built around.

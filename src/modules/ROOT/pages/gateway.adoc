= Gateway

A *gateway* is a xref:design-pattern.adoc[*design*] or xref:architectural-pattern.adoc[*architectural pattern*].
It is an element that encapsulates access to another system or resource, usually an external one, behind a single point
of contact. Callers depend on the gateway's interface rather than on the details of whatever it fronts.

The pattern is closely related to the xref:wrapper.adoc[*wrapper*] and xref:adapter-pattern.adoc[*adapter*]
patterns. All three place an intermediary between a caller and something it depends on, and the terms are often
used loosely and somewhat interchangeably in practice. A gateway is typically distinguished by its focus on
_access_ — routing, protocol translation, or authentication for calls in and out of a system — rather than on
reconciling incompatible interfaces or simplifying a complex one.

Gateways are common at system boundaries, such as an *API gateway* fronting a set of internal services, or a
*payment gateway* abstracting away the proprietary APIs of multiple third-party payment service providers. Gateways
are also commonly put in front of *legacy subsystems* to ease their integration with modernized services.

= Maintainability

*Maintainability* is the degree of effectiveness and efficiency with which a product or system can be modified to
improve it, to correct defects, or to adapt it to changes in its environment or requirements. It is a *non-runtime*
xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*], observable only as a system changes over time, rather than while it
runs.

ISO/IEC 25010 composes maintainability from five sub-characteristics: xref:modularity.adoc[*modularity*],
xref:reusability.adoc[*reusability*], xref:analysability.adoc[*analysability*], xref:modifiability.adoc[*modifiability*],
and xref:testability.adoc[*testability*]. Together these describe how easily a change can be understood, isolated, made,
and verified without breaking something else. A highly modular system with well-covered tests supports change far more
cheaply than one where every part depends on every other part.

Maintainability tends to decline over a system's life unless it is actively defended, a decay generally called
xref:software-rot.adoc[*software rot*] or *bit rot*. xref:refactoring.adoc[*Refactoring*], keeping
xref:technical-debt.adoc[*technical debt*] in check, and following xref:design-principle.adoc[*design principles*]
such as xref:solid.adoc[*SOLID*] help to sustain maintainability.

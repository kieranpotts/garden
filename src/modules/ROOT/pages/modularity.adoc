= Modularity

*Modularity* is the degree to which a system or program is composed of discrete, independent xref:module.adoc[*modules*],
such that a change to one has minimal impact on the others. It is a xref:quality-attribute.adoc[*quality attribute*] and,
per ISO/IEC 25010, a sub-characteristic of xref:maintainability.adoc[*maintainability*]. A highly modular system is
easier to understand, change, and extend because the effects of a change stay local to the module being changed.

Modularity is closely tied to xref:coupling.adoc[*coupling*] and xref:cohesion.adoc[*cohesion*]. A system achieves
high modularity by keeping coupling between modules low and cohesion within each module high, so that responsibilities
are grouped sensibly and dependencies between them are minimized. xref:modular-design.adoc[*Modular design*] is the
practice of deliberately pursuing this structure.

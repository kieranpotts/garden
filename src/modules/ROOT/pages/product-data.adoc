= Product data

*Product data* is data whose values are composed of several fixed attributes, all present at once. It's also known as a
*record*, a *struct*, or a *tuple*. An address, for example, is product data. It has a name, a street, a street
number, a city, and a zip code, and a given address value carries all of these attributes together, not just one of
them.

Product data takes its name from the mathematical notion of a product type. The type of an address is the
cartesian product of the types of its individual fields.

This is the natural counterpart to xref:sum-data.adoc[*sum data*], where a value is exactly one of several distinct
alternatives rather than a combination of several fields at once.

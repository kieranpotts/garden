= Modular design

Modular design is a software design approach that emphasizes the separation of
concerns and the creation of self-contained, reusable components.

A key focus of modular design is to hide *xref:complexity.adoc[complexity]*
through *xref:abstraction.adoc[abstraction]* and
*xref:encapsulation.adoc[encapsulation]*, and to support the *[reusability]*
of units of code. A well-modularized system keeps
xref:coupling.adoc[*coupling*] between modules low and
xref:cohesion.adoc[*cohesion*] within each module high. Keeping that coupling low
is the practice of xref:decoupling.adoc[*decoupling*].

= Quality attributes

Quality attributes are the *xref:cross-cutting-concern.adoc[cross-cutting concerns]* that affect the overall operation of a software system. They are the primary forces that drive the *xref:architecture-and-design.adoc[architecture and design]* of a system.

Quality attributes are specified as *xref:non-functional-requirements.adoc[non-functional requirements]* and include:

* *xref:availability.adoc[Availability]*
* *xref:capacity.adoc[Capacity]*
* *xref:complexity.adoc[Complexity]*
* *xref:compliance.adoc[Compliance]*
* *xref:conceptual-integrity.adoc[Conceptual integrity]*
* *xref:consistency.adoc[Consistency]*
* *xref:data-integrity.adoc[Data integrity]*
* *xref:data-retention.adoc[Data retention]*
* *xref:disaster-recovery.adoc[Disaster recovery]*
* *xref:durability.adoc[Durability]*
* *xref:evolvability.adoc[Evolvability]*
* *xref:fault-tolerance.adoc[Fault tolerance]*
* *xref:latency.adoc[Latency]*
* *xref:observability.adoc[Observability]*
* *xref:partition-tolerance.adoc[Partition tolerance]*
* *xref:performance.adoc[Performance]*
* *xref:privacy.adoc[Privacy]*
* *xref:redundancy.adoc[Redundancy]*
* *xref:scalability.adoc[Scalability]*
* *xref:security.adoc[Security]*
* *xref:visibility.adoc[Visibility]*

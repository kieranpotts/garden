= Forward-deployed engineer (FDE)

:link-palantir-delta:     https://blog.palantir.com/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-palantir-forward-deployed-software-engineer-45ef2de257b1

*A forward-deployed engineer (FDE)* is a software engineer embedded with a single customer. The distinguishing feature is
that an FDE ships working software directly into the customer's production environment.

The role originated at Palantir, which found that selling a data platform to intelligence and defense agencies did not
work as a straightforward product sale. Production data was classified, schemas were undocumented, and the real
workflows were invisible from Palantir's headquarters. The company's response was to send engineers into the customer
organization, instead of shipping software to it. Internally these engineers were called "deltas", and until around 2016
Palantir employed more of them than it did conventional product engineers.

Palantir's own framing of the split is that a product engineer's focus is "one capability, many customers," while an
FDE's is "one customer, many capabilities." An FDE's output is scoped to a single customer account, rather than to
the vendor's general product.

FDEs typically work on the customer's premises, embedded with the xref:domain-expert.adoc[*domain experts*] who use the
software, so they can xref:discovery.adoc[*discover*] how best to adapt the software to their specific needs. FDEs have
an unusual level of exposure to how users really operate, giving them a feedback loop measured in hours and days, rather
than release cycles.

For this reason, FDE is a well-trodden path into product and founding roles.

= Requirements

Requirements specify a condition or capability needed by a user to solve a problem or achieve an objective. They are typically split into *xref:functional-requirements.adoc[functional requirements]* – _what_ the system should do – and *xref:non-functional-requirements.adoc[non-functional requirements]* – _how well_ it should do it.

The discipline of eliciting, analyzing, specifying, and validating requirements is called *xref:requirements-engineering.adoc[requirements engineering]*.

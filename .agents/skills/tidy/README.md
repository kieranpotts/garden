# Tidy

General gardening — a freeform, judgment-driven tidy-up pass over one or more entries.

## What it does

The everyday secateurs work that doesn't fit a single named operation: trimming waffle, smoothing awkward phrasing, moving a paragraph to a better home within the page, dropping repetition, tightening headings and lists. It makes small, self-evidently-good edits directly and reports them, so you can scan and undo any you don't want. Anything bigger — moving content between pages, merging or splitting entries, broken links, a full style pass — it hands off to `/graft`, `/split`, `/prune`, `/tend`, `/cultivate`, or `/fertilize` rather than doing it under the guise of tidying.

## How to invoke

> Tidy agent.adoc.

> Give this page a once-over — trim the waffle and move things about.

> Clean up this section.
